### CLIM-000025: Global closure of boundary-driven abyssal upwelling
- **Subfield:** Ocean circulation and heat uptake
- **Open:** Determine how much deep-water upwelling occurs in thin turbulent boundary layers along slopes. Scale local observations to basin transports while closing the energy budget.
- **Status:** A 2024 dye experiment directly observed strong diapycnal upwelling in one submarine canyon; global prevalence and parameterization remain open.
- **Type:** experimental
- **Why:** Connects small-scale mixing to global overturning.
- **Key works:** Wynne-Cattanach et al. (2024), [“Observations of diapycnal upwelling within a sloping submarine canyon”](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-024-07411-2).
- **Related:** Stratified boundary-layer turbulence; homogenization over rough topography.
